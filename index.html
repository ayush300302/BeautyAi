📖 Evaluation Methodology & Independent Ground Truth

BeautyAI uses a deterministic attribute-based relevance heuristic $R(u, p) \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ to compute independent ground-truth relevance labels. Crucially, relevance labels are calculated completely independently from the recommendation system's final score formula, preventing circular evaluation bias. Precision@K, Recall@K, and position-sensitive NDCG@K are evaluated across $K \in \{3, 5, 10\}$ using a representative suite of skin profiles.
